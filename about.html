About the studio

Editors first,
everything else second.

Editkaro.in started as a two-person edit bay cutting gaming montages for friends. It's now a full studio — editors, colorists, and sound designers working across nine formats for brands who need video that performs, not just looks nice.

Why we exist

Mission

Give every brand — from a solo creator to a performance-marketing team — access to edit, grade, and sound quality that used to require an in-house post team. One brief in, a platform-ready master out.

Become the default edit bay for social-first video: the studio teams call first because they know the pacing, the platform specs, and the turnaround will already be right.
